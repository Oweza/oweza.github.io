# 📋 AGILE TEAM CHARTER

## 🎯 1. Purpose and Core Values

- **Purpose:** To strengthen interns' technical and software testing capabilities through practical, project-based learning while preparing them for employment in the technology industry.
- **Core Values:** Promoting continuous learning, professional integrity, teamwork, mutual trust, and the delivery of high-quality software through DevOps practices.

## 💬 2. Communication Guidelines

- **Communication Platforms:**
  - **Microsoft Teams:** The primary platform for team collaboration, discussions, and project meetings.
  - **SmarterMail:** Used for internal correspondence and scheduling.
  - **Click-up:** Used for internal correspondence and scheduling.
  

- **📅 Weekly Scrum Meetings:**
  - **Monday:** 11:00 AM.
  - **Thursday:** 10:00 AM.
  - These meetings are used to discuss progress, identify challenges, review tasks, and coordinate upcoming activities.

- **Response Times:** The facilitator addresses questions and provides assistance immediately or within one hour.

## 👥 3. Team Roles and Responsibilities

- **Interns:** Responsible for developing technical skills, completing assigned tasks, actively participating in learning activities, and demonstrating competence in industry-standard tools and practices.
- **Facilitator:** Responsible for providing mentorship, offering technical guidance, monitoring progress, and assessing interns' performance and understanding.

## ✅ 4. Definition of Done (DoD)

A task is regarded as complete when the following requirements have been met:

1. The developed code or test script runs successfully.
2. The intern completes an individual demonstration with the facilitator, showcasing their technical knowledge and understanding of the sprint objectives.
